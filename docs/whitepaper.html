Duck Bank

Short loans to named shops on the XRP Ledger.
One shop per vault. Lenders hold the vault MPT share.
Beside each loan, the shop's XRP sits in an escrow to the broker.

QAK: 1,000,000,000 supply, issued on FirstLedger in Q4 2026. Issuer blackholed. Issuer half in two escrows.

Lending: built on XLS-65 (Single Asset Vault) and XLS-66 (Lending Protocol). Mainnet deposits open only after SingleAssetVault, LendingProtocol and LendingProtocolV1_1 are enabled on mainnet. On 8 October 2026 none of them is.

1. The desk

Duck Bank lends RLUSD to a named shop for a short, fixed term: stock for a season, a counter, a delivery. Each shop gets its own closed-ended vault and its own loan broker on the XRP Ledger. Lenders deposit RLUSD into that vault during its subscription window and receive the vault MPT share, the receipt for that shop only. If the shop misses, the loss is written down on that vault. The next shop is a different vault.

QAK is the app token. It lists a shop, sets the fee cut, orders the deposit queue and carries one vote per token on the listing and the cap. These are rules of the Duck Bank app. The ledger knows nothing about them, and no QAK ever stands as loan cover.

Beside the loan, a shop that holds XRP locks it in an escrow to the broker wallet before LoanSet (section 5). XLS-66 does not see that escrow. The broker's bot finishes and sells it if the shop misses, which is an app step, not a ledger step. A shop with no XRP can list only on a small cover-only path.

2. The ledger model

Every field, flag and error below is taken from the XLS-65 and XLS-66 specifications (including the closed-ended vault extension and LendingProtocolV1_1).

#TransactionWhat Duck Bank sets
1VaultCreateAsset RLUSD; VaultKind 1 (ClosedEnded) with SubscriptionDate and RedemptionDate; AssetsMaximum = the cap; Scale 6 (IOU range 0–18); flag tfVaultShareNonTransferable. Shop name, city and purpose stay off-ledger.
2LoanBrokerSetOn that VaultID: DebtMaximum = the cap, CoverRateMinimum 15000 (15%), CoverRateLiquidation 100000 (100%), ManagementFeeRate 1000 or 750 (fixed once the broker exists).
3LoanBrokerCoverDepositRLUSD first-loss cover. Only the broker owner can deposit, and only the vault asset (tecWRONG_ASSET otherwise).
4VaultDepositOnly while now ≤ SubscriptionDate; later deposits fail tecEXPIRED. Withdrawals fail tecTOO_SOON until RedemptionDate.
5LoanSetOnly in the investment window. Signed by the broker and by the shop (CounterpartySignature; without it temBAD_SIGNER). StartDate + PaymentInterval × PaymentTotal + 60 s must be ≤ RedemptionDate. GracePeriod ≤ PaymentInterval.
6LoanPayFixed installments, sent only by the borrower (anyone else gets tecNO_PERMISSION). A payment below the periodic payment fails tecINSUFFICIENT_PAYMENT; a late one needs tfLoanLatePayment and settles exactly one installment. On an impaired loan LoanPay works and lifts the impairment; after default it fails tecKILLED.
7LoanManagetfLoanImpair once a payment is overdue; tfLoanDefault once now > NextPaymentDueDate + GracePeriod.
8LoanDeleteWhen PaymentRemaining is 0: the loan is settled or written off.

One vault serves one shop, and never a second. The vault's pseudo-account holds the RLUSD and issues the shares. It cannot receive ordinary payments, so money reaches lenders only through VaultDeposit, LoanPay and, on default, cover moved by LoanManage.

The lender holds the vault MPT share. It is non-transferable, redeemable with VaultWithdraw from RedemptionDate, and it tracks that one vault's assets, including any loss written down on it.

3. The first pool

One shop, a 10,000 RLUSD cap, short term: Kostas Bakery, Athens, stock. Terms: InterestRate 10000 (10% a year), PaymentTotal 3, PaymentInterval 30 days, GracePeriod 7 days. The vault and broker have not been created yet, so there is no VaultID or LoanBrokerID to publish.

Maximum principal under the cap

AssetsMaximum = DebtMaximum = 10,000 RLUSD. XLS-66 counts the expected interest toward both limits. LoanSet fails if AssetsTotal ≥ AssetsMaximum, if AssetsTotal + InterestDue > AssetsMaximum, or if DebtTotal + Principal + InterestDue > DebtMaximum (all tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED). A full 10,000 deposit plus a 10,000 principal therefore cannot be lent.

4. When a shop misses

There is no on-chain collateral and no liquidation. The XLS-66 security section states that the protocol does not offer on-chain algorithmic protection against default. What the ledger does, after the grace period, is LoanManage with tfLoanDefault:

DefaultCovered = min(DebtTotal × CoverRateMinimum × CoverRateLiquidation, DefaultAmount, CoverAvailable)
Vault loss = DefaultAmount − DefaultCovered, written down on that vault only.

Worked on the first pool, if nothing is repaid: DefaultCovered = min(9,999.98 × 15% × 100%, 9,999.98, 1,500.00) = 1,500.00 RLUSD of cover moved into the vault, and 8,499.98 RLUSD written down on that vault. No other vault is touched. The XLS-66 text books expected interest into AssetsTotal at LoanSet; on devnet with LendingProtocolV1_1 the ledger booked interest only as payments arrived (seen in our devnet run). The app counts expected interest toward the caps and the cover, which is the stricter bound. Redemption still opens on RedemptionDate even if the loan is late.

The shop's XRP escrow (section 5) does not change this formula. Whatever the bot's sale brings in reaches the vault only as a LoanPay that the shop has signed, before the default; the rest is still written down.

5. The XRP escrow beside the loan

Escrow fields and errors are from the XRPL Escrow documentation; the swap math is from the AMM (XLS-30) and DEX documentation. XLS-66 does not take this escrow as collateral, and it never sells it. Everything the ledger does not enforce is marked app rule.

#StepWhoWhat the ledger checks
1EscrowCreate before LoanSetshop (Xaman)XRP Amount in drops to Destination = the broker wallet; Condition PREIMAGE-SHA-256 (the only condition type XRPL supports); CancelAfter required for a conditional escrow; optional FinishAfter before it. The broker makes the preimage and keeps the fulfillment off the app.
2Find the escrowappledger_entry by owner + sequence: Destination, Condition, Amount and CancelAfter must match. LoanSet stays blocked in the app until this passes, and until the opening ratio is at least 150% (app rules).
3LoanSetbroker + shopAs in section 2. The escrow is not a LoanSet field.
4Watch the ratioapp (read-only)amm_info and book_offers on XRP/RLUSD (mainnet read; XRP/DUSD on devnet, or "no market").
Ratio = what the escrowed XRP would fetch now if sold (AMM formula, order-book walk, or both, whichever is best) ÷ RLUSD still owed after cover. "Owed after cover" = DefaultAmount − DefaultCovered from the ledger's own default formula, so only the cover the ledger would actually move counts.
Add line 150% · default line 120% (app rules; both shown live on the pool page).

Under 150%, the shop has a window (config: 72 hours) to add a second escrow (an existing escrow cannot be increased: the only escrow transactions are EscrowCreate, EscrowFinish and EscrowCancel) or to LoanPay the balance down. If the window ends under 120%, the bot runs. On the first pool's planned figures (8,499.98 RLUSD owed after 1,500.00 of cover), 150% needed about 9,010 XRP on mainnet XRP/RLUSD at 13:00 EEST on 8 October 2026 (validated ledger 107514201), and 120% about 7,207 XRP. This moves with the market.

The four sale steps (each shown as done or stuck)

StepTransactionNotes
1. MarkLoanManage tfLoanImpairThe ledger allows it only once a payment is overdue. A price breach alone, with payments current, waits for the next due date.
2. FinishEscrowFinish + fulfillmentOnly after FinishAfter and before CancelAfter. Any account may submit it; the XRP goes to the broker.
3. SellOfferCreate tfImmediateOrCancel + tfSellFills against the AMM and the order book, with a 3% limit below the quote (config). Nothing rests on the book.
4. LoanPaythe shop's signed LoanPayLoanPay is borrower-only, so the shop signs it in Xaman without submitting. The bot sends the shop exactly that amount from the proceeds and submits the shop's LoanPay. A vault deposit is not a route: VaultDeposit after SubscriptionDate fails tecEXPIRED, and the pseudo-account cannot receive payments.

Then LoanManage tfLoanDefault for the remainder, once the ledger allows it. Any shortfall is written down on that vault. The pool page derives all four steps from ledger reads (Loan flags, escrow entries, validated transactions); the browser holds no keys. The bot is a broker-side Node script.

No XRP: the cover-only path

A shop with no XRP lists on the cover-only path with a cap of at most 1,000 RLUSD (config) and a file kept on the desk, off-ledger. It has no escrow; on default only RLUSD cover reaches the vault.

8. Status and gating

XLS-65/66 need three amendments: SingleAssetVault, LendingProtocol and LendingProtocolV1_1. A rippled release is not activation: an amendment counts only once validators enable it on that network. The app's Status page runs the read-only feature request on each network. On 8 October 2026: mainnet none enabled, testnet none enabled, devnet all three enabled.

  • Mainnet: vault lending is off in the build itself, regardless of amendment status. No mainnet deposits.
  • Testnet: status only.
  • Devnet: the real transactions, once all three amendments read enabled. RLUSD does not exist on devnet, so the app uses a labelled test IOU from a configured test issuer; with none configured, devnet actions stay disabled.

Devnet run of the escrow path (8 October 2026, 13:13–13:35 EEST, DUSD test token, cap 50). The shop escrowed 15 + 9 XRP to the broker wallet. The app kept LoanSet blocked at a 127.6% ratio and opened it at 176%. After a price drop and a missed installment, the bot marked the loan, finished both escrows, sold 24 XRP for 31.53 DUSD and paid 24.99 DUSD as the shop's own signed LoanPay. LoanPay worked on the impaired loan; nothing was blocked. The remaining 6.55 DUSD was below one installment and stayed undelivered. After the next miss, the default moved 3.75 DUSD of cover, and 21.24 DUSD was written down on that vault. The broker's LoanPay (tecNO_PERMISSION), the shop's early EscrowCancel (tecNO_PERMISSION), a VaultDeposit after SubscriptionDate (tecEXPIRED) and LoanPay after default (tecKILLED) were rejected as expected; after CancelAfter, a third party cancelled leftover escrows and the XRP went back to the shop. Every hash is on the app's Status page.

Xaman is the wallet. Its network list includes DEVNET, so sign requests can be forced to devnet. Xaman support for the XLS-65/66 transaction types is not verified, and Xaman does not document producing a LoanSet CounterpartySignature, so the shop's counter-signature cannot yet be collected in the app. EscrowCreate is a standard mainnet transaction type, and Xaman's developer documentation says it accepts all mainnet transaction types. Without an API key, the app runs a wallet labelled mock that signs nothing.

9. Risks

  • Clawback. The RLUSD issuer has clawback enabled. It can claw back RLUSD from a vault and broker cover above the minimum.
  • No on-chain collateral. Loans are unsecured on the ledger. The shop's XRP escrow sits beside the loan; XLS-66 does not take it as collateral.
  • No ledger liquidation. On default only RLUSD cover, up to the rates, reaches the vault. The bot's XRP sale is an app step: it may fall short (thin book, price gap, the 3% limit), it needs the shop's signed LoanPay, and the broker can finish an escrow at any time it holds the fulfillment. The rest is written down on that vault.
  • Lock-up. Withdrawals are blocked from SubscriptionDate to RedemptionDate.
  • Interest booking. The XLS-66 text books expected interest at LoanSet; devnet booked it as paid. The app counts expected interest toward the caps.
  • XRP locked. A repaying shop's XRP stays in escrow until CancelAfter. If LoanSet never happens, it also stays until CancelAfter.
  • Software. The code is not audited.
